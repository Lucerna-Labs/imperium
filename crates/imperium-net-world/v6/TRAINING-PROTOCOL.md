# Revision 6 local training and conditional admission freeze

Frozen before the first optimization run. Parent source is the unchanged v4
world; v6 has its own graph and does not claim to reproduce v4's absent trainer.
All outputs stay unsealed and `local_diagnostic`.

Inputs are `catalog.json`, `SOURCE-AND-SPLIT-FREEZE.json`, `fixtures.json` and
`training-contexts.json`. Their exact hashes and the implementation commit
are recorded by the training receipt. Catalog sources are pinned official RFC
texts used for citations; training material is original typed factual
paraphrases and explicit synthetic VM deployment declarations.

The 37-node/86-edge source graph has positive, negative and cold/untrained
relations. Every known training edge must have a true condition bundle and
reachable source in at least one frozen training context. Negative edges are
repelled separately; false/unknown conditions cannot become positive updates.
The withheld SCTP control is never fitted or admitted. New endpoint examples
inside a declared CIDR exercise variable instantiation; they are not claims
of unseen-mechanism neural generalization.

Full geometry is 48D. Seed 73161; 1600 epochs; learning rate 0.01; negative
margin 0.5. Sample only a frozen applicable context for each edge update.
The 4D spatial observer is fit by SVD on training nodes only and never
participates in admission.

Fixed local gates: all 26 declared conditional controls have the expected
complete/absent path, zero negative false paths and zero represented positive
false rejects; the conditions-ignored control produces at least one false
path; maximum positive squared edge distance <= 0.02; minimum fitted negative
squared edge distance >= 0.45; orthonormal observer error <= 1e-10; cold edges
absent from all optimizer inputs. Report raw coordinate false proximity
separately: coordinates may not authorize an edge or override conditions.

This verifies static conditional-world consistency and local geometry
continuity. It makes no claim of a learned security advantage over exact
typed relationships, accelerator acceptance, a completed network catalog,
physical-host service authorization or a seal. Native Rust loading/admission,
real fact-producer regressions and full VM passage/failure controls remain
mandatory before reporting functional packet filtering.

Every run has a fresh output directory. Preserve failures; do not change a
gate after reading its evaluation. World packaging occurs only when every
local gate passes, and its hashes bind graph, conditions and trained geometry.
