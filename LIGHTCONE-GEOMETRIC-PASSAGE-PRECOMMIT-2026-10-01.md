## Packet boundary

Kernel input/output/forward interception must hold exact IP bytes until
admission completes, including direct sockets and loopback. Native mesh sends
and TUN writes must have no independent bypass. Queue capacity is bounded;
no nft bypass or NFQUEUE fail-open flag is permitted. Reader loss, bad framing,
capture truncation, unknown/checksum-incomplete form, malformed packet,
unavailable geometry, missing required relationship and exhaustion cannot
produce a transferable admitted packet. Keep the kernel hold/block on reader
exit rather than automatically reopening the network.

All privilege-bearing runtime tests use owned fresh VMs or private namespaces.
No real-host route, firewall, interface or existing VM is changed during
development. An explicit production invocation must bind its actual rules and
world identity; no global protection status may be inferred from a VM result.
